## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-precision, executive-tier fintech experience tailored for personal wealth intelligence and automated capital optimization. Built on an atmospheric foundation of deep cosmic slate and obsidian navy, it balances institutional fiscal discipline with the fluidity of ambient artificial intelligence.

The visual direction combines **Glassmorphism** with **Minimalist Modern FinTech**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Multi-tiered slate surfaces with subtle blur, translucent layering, and micro-borders establish a calm, focused environment where complex financial telemetry feels light and navigable.
- **Clarity & Decisiveness:** Financial metrics demand zero ambiguity. High-contrast typography paired with luminous, semantically bound emerald and coral pigments ensure that gains, losses, and runway calculations are instantly distinguishable at a glance.
- **Cognitive Co-Pilot Presence:** Violet, indigo, and electric cyan accents are strictly reserved for machine-learning interventions, automated budget forecasting, and generative financial advisories.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Layouts prioritize frictionless legibility across alphanumeric figures, Latin typography, and Thai script (Prompt/Sarabun cadence), honoring baseline alignments and diacritic clearances without breaking tabular harmony.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative dark canvas punctuated by semantic financial signals and specialized intelligence markers.

### Canvas & Structural Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F17`):** The foundational substrate for full-screen dashboards, canvas edges, and underlying structural grids.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111827`):** Side navigation, standard container shells, and baseline module backdrops.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Interactive cards, hover states, and layered analytic modules.
- **Structural Outlines (`#1F2937` & `#334155`):** Subdued boundaries used to separate analytical zones without visual noise.

### Functional & Semantic Accents
- **Primary Financial Signal (`#10B981` / `#059669`):** Represents capital growth, net positive savings, investments, verified inflows, and primary commit actions.
- **Secondary Expense Signal (`#EF4444` / `#F87171`):** Represents outflows, liability warnings, over-budget indicators, and debt vectors.
- **AI Intelligence Spectrum (`#8B5CF6` / `#6366F1` / `#06B6D4`):** Denotes automated actions, predictive analytics, smart categorizations, and LLM advice streams.
- **Text & Numeric Hierarchy:**
  - High-Emphasis (`#F8FAFC`): Numerical balances, lead metrics, titles.
  - Medium-Emphasis (`#CBD5E1`): Section labels, standard body copy, table content.
  - Low-Emphasis (`#94A3B8`): Metadata, secondary units, currency descriptors (THB / ฿).

## Typography

The typographic system emphasizes clean numerical legibility and fluid bilingual rendering.

### Primary Families & Fallbacks
- **Latin & Numerical Interface:** `Inter` handles headlines, metrics, and conversational UI with precision geometric curves and balanced vertical proportions. All numerical components leverage `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to guarantee monospaced tabular alignment across banking balances and decimal ledgers.
- **Thai Typography Strategy:** For environments rendering Thai content, the typographic stack relies on system and web fallbacks configured as: `'Inter', 'Prompt', 'Sarabun', -apple-system, sans-serif`. For loopless modern displays and balance headers, `Prompt` preserves high synergy with `Inter`. For prolonged textual financial statements, budgets, and policy reports, `Sarabun` provides looped legibility for sustained scanning.
- **Technical & Computational Data:** `JetBrains Mono` serves ledger codes, transaction IDs, smart rule syntax, and ISO currency tickers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate multi-pane analytics and micro-data visualizations.

### Grid System & Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid with `2rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates persistent left navigation (260px fixed width), flexible multi-card analytical streams, and dynamic right-side AI intelligence drawers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Collapses the primary navigation into an icon rail or top app bar; AI insights reflow under financial metrics.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stream stack with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. All dual-pane summary matrices drop to sequential vertical hierarchies. Sticky horizontal scroll containers are permitted strictly for time-series filter chips and categorization tags.

### Internal Spacing Cadence
- `space-xs` (4px): Used between icon badges and accompanying indicator copy.
- `space-sm` (8px): Inner element spacing for button icons, compact input fields, and pill chips.
- `space-md` (16px): Structural internal cell padding across tabular views and card bodies.
- `space-lg` (24px): Primary separation gap between stacked modules, chart legends, and headers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-level macro clearance for top-level dashboard domains.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through translucent glassmorphic layering, subtle ambient luminosity, and thin light-catching boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure solid `#0B0F17`. Non-interactive baseline surface.
- **Layer 1 Surface Containers (Cards & Panels):**
  - Background: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.36)`.
- **Layer 2 Overlays & Flyouts (Modals, AI Drawers, Dropdowns):**
  - Background: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Ambient Edge Glows & Semantics
- **Positive Inflow Focus:** Border tinting dynamically blends into `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` with a faint interior gradient glow `radial-gradient(ellipse at top left, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08), transparent 70%)`.
- **Expense Warnings:** Outlined with `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)` accompanied by a subtle warning wash for limit breaches.
- **AI Intelligence State:** Cards bearing automated advice carry an iridescent micro-stroke gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4), rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4))` combined with a faint soft purple backlight.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances contemporary software polish with executive reliability. Roundedness factor 2 sets base interface elements to a smooth 0.5rem radius, providing a tactile, modern feel while keeping analytical charts, data tables, and metrics orderly.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, standard buttons, dropdown triggers, and notification toasts.
- **Container Elements (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Financial summary cards, analytic chart wrappers, bottom sheets, and dialog surfaces.
- **Hero & Floating Shells (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Onboarding modals, AI conversation pods, and omni-search palettes.
- **Pills (Full Radius):** Financial category tags, status badges, THB currency markers, and interactive segmented controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Financial Action (Savings / Deposits / Create):** Background in `#10B981`, text in `#0B0F17` (demibold for maximum contrast), radius 0.5rem. Hover: `#059669`. Focus ring: 2px offset `#10B981`.
- **Secondary / Glass Action:** Background in `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`, text in `#F8FAFC`, border 1px solid `#334155`. Hover: `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.8)`.
- **AI Smart Trigger:** Background in `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #8B5CF6 50%, #06B6D4 100%)`, text in `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner glow.

### Cards & Analytic Modules
- Built using Layer 1 surface glass. Internal layout uses `space-md` or `space-lg` padding.
- Card titles feature small uppercase category tracking (`11px`, letter-spacing `0.04em`, `#94A3B8`).
- Metric cards showcase high-contrast figures (`#F8FAFC`, tabular numerals) coupled with dynamic trend badges (+X.X% in `#10B981` chip, -X.X% in `#EF4444` chip).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`.
- Resting border: 1px solid `#334155`. Focused border: 1.5px solid `#10B981` with soft focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- Currency prefix inputs display the Thai Baht sign (`฿`) or ISO string (`THB`) locked in neutral slate `#94A3B8` with fixed spacing to avoid overlap.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Positive Badge:** `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` background, text `#10B981`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Negative / Debt Badge:** `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)` background, text `#EF4444`, border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`.
- **AI Recommendation Badge:** `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` background, text `#A78BFA`, border `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`. Incorporates a micro sparkle or cyan glyph icon.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes maintain a 0.25rem corner radius, `#1E293B` background, and checked fill of `#10B981`.
- Toggles utilize an obsidian track (`#1E293B`) transitioning to `#10B981` on active states, with a crisp off-white (`#F8FAFC`) circular thumb.

### AI Insight Banners & Drawer Components
- Encased in a frosted slate housing with an ethereal purple-to-cyan left-hand border accent (3px).
- Generative summary statements use regular body sizing (`14px`, `#CBD5E1`) with highlighted financial tokens rendered in monospaced or bold text. Actionable suggestion links sit in `#06B6D4` with an underline-on-hover interaction.
